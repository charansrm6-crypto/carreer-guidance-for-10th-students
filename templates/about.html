About CareerPath

What is CareerPath?

CareerPath is a student-focused guidance platform designed to help 10th-class students understand their education and career choices clearly and confidently.

We aim to simplify complex decisions by presenting practical information about academic streams, career options, courses, scholarships, and entrance examinations in an accessible format. ECE is a special focus, with general guidance on electronics, communication, embedded systems, VLSI, IoT, networking, and related study paths.

Why this website exists

Many students feel confused after 10th class because they have multiple streams and pathways to choose from. CareerPath exists to help students explore their interests, compare options, and build a realistic future plan.

What students can learn

  • After-10th education routes
  • Career options and job opportunities
  • Course and higher education information
  • Scholarship guidance
  • Entrance exam awareness
  • ECE study and career guidance, including possible diploma and vocational routes

CareerPath values

  • Student-friendly guidance
  • Clear decision support
  • Practical education advice
  • Accessible information
