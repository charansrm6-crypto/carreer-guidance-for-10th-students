# User Guide

## Home page
Use the home page to understand CareerPath, learn about education options, and access student registration or login.

## Registration
Students can register with their full name, email, class, phone number, and a password. Duplicate email addresses are blocked.

## Login and dashboard
After logging in, users are redirected to the dashboard, which links to roadmaps, careers, courses, scholarships, entrance exams, ECE guidance, the team, About, and Contact. Logout uses a CSRF-protected POST form.

## Content areas
- Roadmaps: stream-specific guidance and detail pages
- Careers: job and higher-education information
- Courses: course details and eligibility
- Scholarships: scholarship information and eligibility notes
- Exams: exam information and preparation context
- ECE guidance: general pathways and study/career areas; admission and lateral-entry eligibility must be checked with official sources
- Team: the five-member team section
- Contact: message form for support requests

## WhatsApp group
The footer includes a WhatsApp group link that opens in a new browser tab.

## Content accuracy
Scholarship, entrance-exam, admission, and career information can change. Verify eligibility, dates, benefits, course recognition, and application instructions with the official scheme, examination, or institution website.

## Contact messages
Students and visitors can submit the contact form. The staff-only message page and Django Admin require an authorized staff account.
